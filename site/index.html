How it works

Fuel surcharges reset weekly. Most carriers change on Monday, pegged to the diesel index published the prior Thursday. This board re-checks every morning and records each change.

Sources

Each carrier’s own published fuel surcharge, collected from its website. Diesel: Natural Resources Canada, weekly average retail price across seven BC cities, taxes included.
